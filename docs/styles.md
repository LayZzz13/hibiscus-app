# Hibiscus Grade Styles

This document defines the intended visual identity and behavior of the sixteen built-in Hibiscus Grade styles.

These descriptions are creative targets rather than exact numeric specifications.

Implementation values may be tuned over time, but each style should continue to preserve the visual identity described here.

All Grade styles should:

- preserve useful highlight and shadow detail;
- avoid unnecessary global exposure shifts;
- avoid visible artificial grain or camera-style degradation;
- preserve image clarity;
- keep neutral colors reasonably stable unless a style intentionally shifts them;
- avoid excessive skin-tone contamination;
- remain visually usable throughout their two-dimensional Style pad.

## Style Pad Model

Each style has a designed center point.

The horizontal axis explores **Color**:

- left: more restrained interpretation;
- right: richer or more expressive interpretation.

The vertical axis explores **Tone**:

- up: lighter, softer, and more open;
- down: deeper, denser, and more contrast-focused.

These axes are not simple saturation and exposure controls.

Each style should interpret the same gestures according to its own color identity.

---

## Pure

**Character:** Neutral · Clean · Natural

Pure is the baseline Hibiscus Grade.

It should improve tonal polish without making the photograph look obviously filtered.

### Center

- neutral white balance
- natural saturation
- mild contrast
- clean blacks
- gentle highlight rolloff
- minimal hue bias

### Color axis

**Left**
- slightly restrained chroma
- very neutral rendering

**Right**
- naturally richer color
- slightly stronger separation between major hues

Avoid artificial saturation.

### Tone axis

**Up**
- lighter mids
- softer contrast
- more open shadows

**Down**
- deeper mids
- firmer contrast
- slightly denser blacks

Pure should remain the reference against which the other styles are judged.

---

## Air

**Character:** Bright · Cool · Open

Air creates a lighter and more spacious image without becoming washed out.

### Center

- slightly cool balance
- lower contrast
- restrained saturation
- open shadows
- clean pale blues
- protected highlights

### Color axis

Left moves toward a more neutral, desaturated interpretation.

Right emphasizes cool blue/cyan character while remaining clean.

### Tone axis

Up becomes brighter and softer.

Down restores more density and black structure.

Do not let Air become foggy or overexposed.

---

## Glow

**Character:** Warm · Luminous · Creamy

Glow is built around warm mids and soft luminous highlights.

### Center

- warm-neutral mids
- creamy whites
- gentle contrast
- soft highlight rolloff
- natural skin response

### Color axis

Left moves toward restrained cream and neutral warmth.

Right moves toward richer golden warmth.

### Tone axis

Up emphasizes luminous highlights and open mids.

Down creates a richer and denser warm image.

Avoid yellow casts and clipped highlights.

---

## Soft

**Character:** Pastel · Gentle · Understated

Soft lowers tonal aggression while preserving clarity.

### Center

- lower contrast
- restrained saturation
- modest black lift
- soft tonal transitions
- preserved detail

### Color axis

Left becomes more neutral and muted.

Right introduces slightly richer pastel color.

### Tone axis

Up becomes lighter and airier.

Down introduces more tonal structure without becoming hard.

Soft must never use blur as its primary effect.

---

## Rich

**Character:** Dense · Full · Colorful

Rich emphasizes depth and color density.

### Center

- deeper blacks
- stronger color separation
- moderate-high contrast
- full but controlled saturation

### Color axis

Left produces a restrained dense look.

Right increases color richness and separation.

### Tone axis

Up opens the shadows and softens density.

Down creates deeper blacks and stronger midtone weight.

Avoid crushed blacks and oversaturation.

---

## Chrome

**Character:** Crisp · Graphic · Chromatic

Chrome emphasizes selective color separation rather than global saturation.

### Center

- slightly cyan-leaning blues
- deeper reds
- restrained greens
- clean neutral mids
- crisp contrast

### Color axis

Left produces a more restrained, neutral Chrome response.

Right increases cyan-blue and red character while continuing to control greens.

### Tone axis

Up creates a cleaner and brighter graphic image.

Down increases density and contrast.

Chrome should never become a generic saturation boost.

---

## Fade

**Character:** Lifted · Muted · Nostalgic

Fade creates a lower-contrast photographic response while retaining structure.

### Center

- lifted black point
- reduced saturation
- mild warm-neutral bias
- softer contrast

### Color axis

Left approaches neutral muted color.

Right introduces slightly stronger tinted color.

### Tone axis

Up becomes lighter and more washed.

Down restores stronger midtone and black structure.

Avoid turning the whole image gray or foggy.

---

## Ember

**Character:** Amber · Peach · Warm

Ember creates warmth through selective orange and amber relationships.

### Center

- warm highlights
- peach mids
- subtle burnt-orange character
- controlled neutral areas

### Color axis

Left moves toward softer peach.

Right moves toward deeper amber and burnt orange.

### Tone axis

Up favors brighter golden warmth.

Down emphasizes richer warm mids and shadows.

Avoid turning skin or neutral surfaces uniformly orange.

---

## Blush

**Character:** Rose · Peach · Gentle

Blush introduces a soft rose-peach bias suited to people and everyday scenes.

### Center

- soft rose mids
- warm peach highlights
- restrained magenta
- gentle contrast

### Color axis

Left stays closer to neutral peach.

Right strengthens rose and blush character.

### Tone axis

Up becomes lighter and softer.

Down becomes richer and slightly denser.

Protect skin from excessive pink or magenta shifts.

---

## Moss

**Character:** Earthy · Olive · Green

Moss emphasizes natural earthy greens rather than applying a global green tint.

### Center

- olive-biased greens
- restrained yellows
- earthy neutral mids
- moderate contrast

### Color axis

Left produces subdued olive tones.

Right increases deeper forest-green character.

### Tone axis

Up becomes softer and lighter.

Down emphasizes earthy density and deeper shadows.

Neutral areas should not become green.

---

## Tide

**Character:** Cyan · Ocean · Cool

Tide creates clean cool color with emphasis on cyan and blue relationships.

### Center

- slightly cool balance
- cyan-blue emphasis
- clean highlights
- controlled saturation

### Color axis

Left moves toward pale cool neutrality.

Right deepens cyan/ocean-blue character.

### Tone axis

Up becomes brighter and cleaner.

Down produces deeper cool tones and stronger contrast.

Avoid turning skin and neutral whites globally cyan.

---

## Dusk

**Character:** Violet Shadows · Warm Light

Dusk creates a subtle cool-shadow / warm-light relationship.

### Center

- blue-violet shadows
- warm mids and highlights
- softer highlight response
- moderate saturation

### Color axis

Left reduces the split-tone relationship.

Right strengthens violet/cool shadows and warm light.

### Tone axis

Up emphasizes open warm light.

Down deepens the cooler shadow character.

The effect should remain photographic rather than theatrical.

---

## Cinema

**Character:** Restrained · Cinematic · Separated

Cinema creates controlled warm/cool separation without relying on an exaggerated teal-orange LUT.

### Center

- slightly cool shadows
- warmer mids
- modestly reduced saturation
- stronger tonal structure

### Color axis

Left approaches a subtle neutral cinematic response.

Right strengthens warm/cool separation.

### Tone axis

Up produces softer cinematic contrast.

Down produces denser blacks and stronger midtone weight.

Avoid obvious teal-orange color casting.

---

## Neon

**Character:** Electric · Cyan · Magenta

Neon is designed primarily for colorful scenes, artificial lighting, and night photography.

### Center

- cyan and magenta emphasis
- strong color separation
- higher vibrance
- controlled highlights

### Color axis

Left produces a cooler cyan-led response.

Right strengthens magenta and richer electric color.

### Tone axis

Up opens bright artificial lights.

Down creates deeper blacks and stronger night contrast.

Avoid clipping saturated lights.

---

## Silver

**Character:** Soft · Monochrome · Smooth

Silver is the gentler Hibiscus monochrome Grade.

### Center

- monochrome conversion
- moderate contrast
- slightly open blacks
- smooth highlight transitions

### Color axis

The horizontal axis modifies the monochrome color-response mix rather than introducing visible color.

Different source hues should translate differently into grayscale.

### Tone axis

Up becomes softer and more open.

Down increases monochrome density.

Silver should remain graceful rather than dramatic.

---

## Ink

**Character:** Graphic · Dark · Monochrome

Ink is the stronger monochrome Grade.

### Center

- deeper blacks
- stronger tonal separation
- higher contrast
- crisp grayscale response

### Color axis

Changes how source hues map into grayscale, allowing different emphasis of skies, skin, foliage, and other colors.

### Tone axis

Up softens the result slightly.

Down creates stronger blacks and a more graphic appearance.

Avoid crushing important shadow detail.
